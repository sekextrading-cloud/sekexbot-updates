SekexBot setup

Three taps and one paste. The phone does the rest by itself. Plug the phone in and connect to Wi-Fi first.

1Install Termux

Termux is the free app SekexBot runs inside.

Download Termux
  1. Tap the button. If the phone warns about the file, tap Download anyway.
  2. Tap Open when the download finishes, then Install.
  3. If the phone says your browser can't install apps: tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, press Back, tap Install.

Do not use the Termux from the Play Store. It does not work.

2Copy the setup line

curl -L https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sekextrading-cloud/sekexbot-updates/main/setup.sh | bash

If the button does nothing, hold your finger on the green text and tap Copy.

3Paste it into Termux

  1. Open the Termux app (black screen with white text). Wait until the text stops moving.
  2. Hold your finger on the black screen, then tap Paste.
  3. Tap the Enter key on the keyboard (the ↵ key).

What happens next

  1. Three small windows pop up, one after another. On each one tap Install, then Done. The first time the phone may ask to allow Termux to install apps: tap Settings, switch it on, press Back.
  2. Then leave the phone alone for 10 to 40 minutes. Keep it plugged in. Do not close Termux.
  3. When it is finished, SekexBot opens by itself. If the phone shows a Battery page for Termux, choose Unrestricted.
  4. In SekexBot, tap Broker keys and enter your Alpaca keys.

Something went wrong? Do step 3 again. It picks up where it stopped. If it fails twice, send a screenshot.
